Knowledge Model

The Knowledge Model is the centralized repository to store and share knowledge on an App (i.e., Package) level. It acts as a semantic layer between your Data Model and App - allowing you to add business logic to your (most likely, technical) data. You can store business definitions - like KPIs, Filters, and Variables - so that your App has a common logic that you and other analysts use.




In most cases, Knowledge Models must be created together with the other Assets - providing varying degrees of functionalities to them.




Create a Knowledge Model using the following details...


Name: OCPM_OrderManagement KM


Data Model Variable (Key): dm_ocpm_ordermanagement

Knowledge Model Type: Base




In the Knowledge Model, go to KPIs. Let's create a KPI using the following details...


Display Name: Total Net Order Value

Description: The total financial value of all goods and services on a sales order item before any taxes, freight charges, discounts, or surcharges are applied.


PQL Formula: SUM ( "o_celonis_SalesOrderItem"."NetAmount" )

Format: Abbreviated (##.#k)

Unit: €

KPI Desired Direction: Up Trend

View

The View is where you build the user interfaces of an App. You can customize Views to create analyses, explore your business processes, and find value opportunities. In many cases, the business users would only interact with Views in their Apps. Due to their versatility, Views can be layered and allow analysts to design persona-specific UIs, each with their own sets of functionality.

Historically, the View is the evolution of the Analysis (another Asset in Studio) and offers more guided interaction to the end users. Views have extra features and components to operationalize data.




A View is always connected to a Knowledge Model, where all business definitions are stored. You can even show metrics from multiple Data Models in a View using different Knowledge Models. Additionally, Views can connect to Action Flows to produce actions (i.e., to execute specific commands using Views). Since Views are being continuously developed, additional analysis elements are progressively added to its capabilities.

Process Orchestration

A Process Orchestration acts as the conductor of your business processes. While Action Flows handle specific, individual tasks, Process Orchestration coordinates these tasks into a cohesive, long-running process. It ensures that every part of the process happens in the right order and at the right time.

Try creating a Process Orchestration using the following details: 

Name: New Process Orchestration

Click the Edit button in the top-right corner. 

Every Process Orchestration needs to begin with a start event. Click "Add event" on the right to add a new event or choose an existing event as your starting event. Most likely, you need to create a new event - click "Create event" in the pop-up window.

Key: MyStartEvent

Label: MyStartEvent

Click Save. Make sure your new event is selected and click "Add". Now you have already completed the first step of every Process Orchestration configuration!

Form

Forms are the dedicated Human-in-the-Loop asset in Studio. They are purpose-built to facilitate specific, point-in-time interactions where a process requires human intelligence, a decision, or manual data entry to proceed. 

In practice, Forms are almost always used in conjunction with Process Orchestration. Waiting for a form to be filled in might pause a Process Orchestration execution - once the user submits their input, the Orchestration Engine receives the data and automatically resumes the workflow.

Try creating a Form, using the following details:

Name: New Form

A drag-and-drop form builder opens up and lets you design your form the way you need it.

Component: any one you like. Try, for example, adding a text field component and configuring it the way you like.

Remember to save your component and then hit the save icon in the top right to save your entire form.

Process Copilot

Process Copilot is a generative AI chatbot within Celonis that enables users to interact with their process data in a conversational manner. Designed to help navigate and optimize business processes, it leverages process mining and automation capabilities to deliver actionable insights and guidance - improving operational efficiency and decision-making.




It's time to try creating the asset for yourself! But before that, you'll have to enable the use of AI Services in your training team. Take note that you'll have to do this if you want access to any AI services in Celonis.

🚨 Enable AI Services

Please ensure to read, understand, and accept the Celonis Artificial Intelligence Addendum in your training environment. Otherwise, you cannot complete the practical exercises in this course. To do so, navigate to "Admin & Settings", and then to "AI Settings":

Here, you have the option to enable AI Services as required:




After you've enabled the AI services,go back to your Package in Studio and create a Process Copilot using the following information...


Name: New Process Copilot

Knowledge Model: OCPM_OrderManagement KM

From Scratch: Process Copilot for the Celonis Platform




System Prompt

Task: You are an expert in analyzing and optimizing the Order Management process within Celonis, focusing on the metric Total Net Order Value. You will be used by catalog order managers, analysts, and operations teams who seek to understand and improve order-related performance metrics. These users will rely on you during their daily operations or strategic planning sessions to identify trends, resolve inefficiencies, and make data-driven decisions.Your task is to provide a clear overview of Total Net Order Value trends, including historical performance, fluctuations, and growth patterns. You should identify key factors influencing this metric, such as order volume, product categories, or customer segments, and detect process inefficiencies, bottlenecks, or anomalies. Additionally, you are expected to compare performance across different dimensions (e.g., regions, customer types, product lines) and recommend actionable steps or process improvements to maximize Total Net Order Value. Present insights and visualizations in an easy-to-understand, conversational manner to support decision-making. Before returning answers to users, always double-check your responses for accuracy to ensure the information provided is correct and reliable.
